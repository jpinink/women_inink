How listings work

Who is listed

Women in INK lists tattoo shops and studios in the Northeast US (New England, New York and New Jersey) and the UK that are women owned. A shop is listed when it says so publicly (its website, Instagram, a post, an interview or the "Women-owned & operated" tag on its Yelp listing) or when the shop tells us itself by adding its listing. We never guess from names or photos.

Listings use public business information only: the shop's name, town, Instagram, website and how they book. We don't copy or host anyone's tattoo photos. "See their work" always goes to the shop's own Instagram.

Who makes this

Women in INK is made by InINK, a flash booking site run by Jess and April, a working tattoo artist in Conway, NH. No shop pays to be listed, and nobody ranks higher for using InINK. Shops that book flash through InINK get a "Book flash" button, and that's the only difference.

Claim, fix or remove a listing

The buttons under "Is this your shop?" are for the shop's owner and the artists who work there.

Claim: use "Claim this listing" on your shop. Once we've confirmed it's you, Jess sets up your InINK page from the listing and sends you a link to take it over.

Fix something or add a flash day: tell us what's wrong or what to add, like hours, artists, how you book, a flash day or a mom owned tag. No account needed.

Remove: use "Remove my listing". No account and no questions about why. It comes down once we confirm it's the shop, and won't be added again.

How we check it's you: before we change or remove anything, we message the shop's own Instagram account (the one on the listing) and ask you to reply, or we reply to an email sent from the shop's own address (the one on its website or listing). Nothing changes until the shop answers. A Mom owned badge is only added at the owner's request.

You can also email jess@ininktattoobooking.com for any of these.

Privacy

What we keep: the public shop details on each listing, and what you send us through a form (your name, email, Instagram and message). We use form details only to answer you and update your listing, and we never sell them.

We count page visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which uses no cookies and doesn't track you across sites. The map uses OpenFreeMap tiles and the fonts come from Google Fonts, so those services see your browser's usual request details.

For shops and people in the UK: we show public business information because we have a legitimate interest in helping people find women owned shops. You can object at any time, and we'll take your listing down. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

To see, correct or delete anything we hold about you or your shop, email jess@ininktattoobooking.com. ININK LLC, New Hampshire, USA, runs this site.

Terms

Listings are based on public information and checked by hand, but shop details change. Check with the shop before you book or visit. Links go to sites we don't run, and their own terms apply there.

If a shop adds its own photos or text, the shop confirms it has the right to share them and lets us show them on this site. If you think something here uses your work without permission, email us and we'll take it down while we look into it.
