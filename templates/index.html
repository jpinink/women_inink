How listings work, privacy and terms

Claim this listing

Once we've confirmed it's you, Jess sets up your InINK page from this listing and sends you a link to take it over. From there you can change anything.

For the shop's owner or artists only. To protect the shop, we confirm it's you before we change anything: we'll message the shop's own Instagram, or reply to an email from the shop's own address. Nothing changes until you answer.

Fix something

Tell us what to change: hours, artists, how you book, a mom owned tag, or anything that's wrong.

For the shop's owner or artists only. To protect the shop, we confirm it's you before we change anything: we'll message the shop's own Instagram, or reply to an email from the shop's own address. Nothing changes until you answer.

Add a flash day

We add it to your listing and to "Flash days this week" when it's coming up. Shops on InINK can just create the flash day there.

For the shop's owner or artists only. To protect the shop, we confirm it's you before we change anything: we'll message the shop's own Instagram, or reply to an email from the shop's own address. Nothing changes until you answer.

Remove my listing

No account and no questions about why. Once we've confirmed it's the shop, your listing comes down and won't be added again.

For the shop's owner or artists only. To protect the shop, we confirm it's you before we change anything: we'll message the shop's own Instagram, or reply to an email from the shop's own address. Nothing changes until you answer.

Add your listing

For women owned tattoo shops and private studios anywhere in the US or the UK. We'll check it and add your shop.
