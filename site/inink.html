About InINK

InINK is a flash booking site for tattoo artists. Women in INK is made by InINK.

How we got started

InINK started in North Conway, New Hampshire. April is a working tattoo artist at The Darling Grey. Jess spent seven years at FareHarbor, the booking platform for tour and activity companies. We love flash, and we wanted to make it as easy as possible for clients to find a design they love and book it on the spot. So we built InINK: a home for every artist's flash, where clients can pick a piece, choose a time and book in one go.

InINK is 100% women owned.

What we do

  • Every artist gets a flash page with all their available designs.
  • Clients pick a design, a size and a time, and pay the deposit in one step. No DM back and forth.
  • Made for how tattooing works: flash days, repeatable or one of a kind designs, sizes shown at real scale.
  • We never use AI to make or change your art, and your work is never used to train AI.

Free for artists, always

Artists pay nothing. No monthly fee and no card fees. A small booking fee is added on the client's side, only when a booking is paid.
